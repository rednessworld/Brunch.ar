OUR STORY

Rooted in El Born.
Inspired by the Coast.

Lumina Brunch is a celebration of Barcelona's vibrant culinary culture. We blend the raw, structural integrity of our industrial loft space with the sun-drenched warmth of the Mediterranean coast.

Our menu is a testament to locally sourced ingredients, crafted into artisan dishes that invite you to linger, savor, and connect.
